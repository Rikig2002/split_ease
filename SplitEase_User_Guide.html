SplitEase User Guide

How to use SplitEase and what it is for

SplitEase helps a small group track shared expenses, split bills fairly, see who owes what, and settle balances without manual math. It is built for trips, roommates, events, and any situation where one person pays for a group.

Purpose

Track shared spending and balance payments in one place.

Best for

Trips, flatmates, friends, events, or team expenses.

Main value

Replaces manual calculations and avoids payment confusion.

Core Features

Groups

Create a group for each trip or shared budget.

Expenses

Add bills, choose who paid, and split them across members.

Categories

Tag spending like meals, transport, entertainment, and more.

Analytics

See total spend, average expense, top categories, and top payers.

Filtering

Search expenses, filter by category, and sort by amount or date.

Settlements

Mark balances as paid once money has actually moved.

How To Use It

1. Create an account and log in

Sign up with your name, email, and password. After that, log in to access your dashboard.

2. Create a group

Open Groups and create a group name like Weekend Trip or Roommates.

3. Add members to the group

For settlement testing or real usage, the group should contain everyone involved in the shared spending.

4. Add expenses

Choose the group, enter the description and amount, pick who paid, select who the expense is split among, and choose a category.

5. Review analytics

The analytics panel shows total expense count, total amount, average spend, top category, category breakdown, and top payers.

6. Filter and search expenses

Use the search bar to find a bill, category pills to narrow results, and the sort dropdown to reorder entries.

7. Settle balances

When the app shows a pending settlement, click Mark Paid after the payment is completed to close it out.

Example Workflow

Example: three friends go on a trip. One person pays for dinner, another pays for transport, and the app automatically calculates the net balance. The group can then see who owes whom and mark the settlement as paid once money is transferred.

Important Notes

  • Expenses must belong to members of the group.
  • Categories improve analytics and make filtering easier.
  • Marking a settlement as paid updates its status in the database.
  • If an older expense shows as Other, it was created before category persistence was fixed.
SplitEase is meant to keep shared money simple, transparent, and easy to close out.
